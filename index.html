Our Culinary Story

Founded in 2010 by award-winning Chef Michael Laurent, Saveur brings a contemporary approach to classic French cuisine. Our philosophy centers on sourcing the finest seasonal ingredients and transforming them with meticulous technique and creative vision.

Each dish tells a story of tradition, innovation, and respect for ingredients. Our sommelier has curated an exceptional wine list featuring both celebrated vineyards and emerging producers to complement your dining experience.
